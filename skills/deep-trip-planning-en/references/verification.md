# Verification Method

“The place exists and is open” is not enough. Verify that it is open on the planned weekday, at the planned hour, including midday breaks, last orders, and temporary-closure notices. Check the venue's official page; a map's current status is not proof for a future date.

Run the shared date checker before routing. Mark public holidays and any three-day-or-longer run of weekends and holidays: they affect transport, lockers, airport congestion, crowds, and holiday operating hours. Also check policy changes that take effect during the trip and local events near planned venues.

For seasonal events, compare the itinerary with the **current forecast window for that exact place**, day by day. If only one date overlaps, make it the priority. State whether the opportunity is on time, marginal, or missed, plus what to recheck three days before departure and where.

Back-solve every golden-hour plan from the required arrival time through local transit, long-distance transit, departure, and wake-up. If it does not work, change transport or remove the activity.

Record a source and checking date for hard facts. If verification is incomplete, say “recheck X days before departure at [official page]” rather than inventing a value. End the plan with a sources section and repeat critical checks seven days before and on departure day.

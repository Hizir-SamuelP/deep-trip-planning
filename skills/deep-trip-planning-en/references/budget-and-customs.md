# Budget and Customs

Separate home-currency costs from destination-currency costs before totaling them. Use sources checked for the trip for exchange assumptions, fares, fees, and customs rules. Identify the one category without a natural ceiling—often shopping—and give it three spending bands plus a hard cap the traveler can keep on their phone.

For customs, cash, payment, and baggage rules, use official border, airline, and issuer sources. Do not rely on a blog for duty-free allowances, liquid limits, batteries, medication, or card acceptance. Put a dated recheck action in the plan when any policy may change.

# Lodging Decisions

Compare lodging on the same all-in basis: room cost, taxes, mandatory fees, transport cost created by the location, cancellation terms, and the real cost of moving. Treat platform amenity copy as a claim to verify, not evidence.

Set a switching threshold before comparison. A new hotel must beat the current one by a defined amount in price, location, reliability, or itinerary fit to justify the cancellation and moving cost. If it does not, state the decision is closed and stop browsing. Use the lodging as the daily routing origin only after the decision is settled.

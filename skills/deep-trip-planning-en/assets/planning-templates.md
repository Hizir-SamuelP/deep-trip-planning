# Planning Templates

Use only the sections that serve the trip. Any changing hard fact needs a source checked for this trip or a dated recheck action and destination.

```markdown

## Hard constraints
- Booked lodging / transport / reservations:
- Daily origin:
- Preference numbers: pace, queue limit, buffer, budget posture, certainty preference
- Entry and health checks: status, official source, latest action date
## Final choices
## N-day overview
## Transit and map execution
## Daily routes
## Best timing and traps
## Budget total
## Booking and recheck countdown
## Packing list
## Emergency card

```

## Daily route

```markdown
### Day N · YYYY-MM-DD (weekday) · [holiday/long-weekend label]
Origin: [lodging/station] · Weather risk: [specific risk]
| Time | Action | Transit/walk | Checked source or recheck action |
|---|---|---|---|
- Must keep:
- May drop:
- Queue rule: after [X] minutes, go to [named alternative].
- Weather fallback: if [condition], do [specific alternative].
- Buffer: [duration] for [delay/rest/exit-finding].
```

## Countdown

```markdown
| Latest date | Action | Source | Owner | Consequence if missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | Visa/travel authorization/arrival registration recheck | Official page | Traveler | May not board |
| YYYY-MM-DD | Free cancellation deadline | Booking confirmation | Traveler | Fee |
| YYYY-MM-DD | Reservation or ticket release | Official booking page | Traveler | Sellout |
| 7 days before | Recheck hours, prices, rules, seasonal window | Official page | Planner | Wasted trip |
| Departure day | Recheck transit and temporary notices | Operator notice | Traveler | Missed flight/detour |
```

## Packing method

Build the list from weather, activities, trip length with laundry access, and carry-on/checked-bag limits. Keep passport, medicine, charging, a change of clothes, and first-night essentials in carry-on. Recheck liquid, battery, lithium, medicine, and activity-equipment rules with the airline and destination. Categorize as carry-on, checked, buy locally, or confirm before departure.

## Emergency card

```markdown
## Emergency card
- Emergency medical: [destination-specific number]; police: [number]; non-emergency police/tourist help: [number or official page]
- Nearest emergency care: [name, address, phone, route]
- Embassy/consulate: [name, address, 24-hour phone, official link]
- Missed flight: [airline local number, counter/rebooking route, latest arrival time]
- Lost passport: [police process, emergency travel-document process, backup-copy location]
- All payment fails: [backup physical card, emergency cash, issuer international number, trusted contact]
```

Fill actual destination numbers and official sources; “call local emergency services” is not an emergency plan.
